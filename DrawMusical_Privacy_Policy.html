Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

How information is handled when you draw, create music, and use our app.

Your creations stay under your control.

Drawings and generated music are processed on your device. Advertising services and Google Play process information needed for their services. You can delete your work and turn off creative reminders.

This policy applies to Draw Musical for Android, package com.wcs.drawing.game.draw.with.music.fun, provided by [DEVELOPER OR COMPANY NAME] (“we”, “us”, or “our”).

1. Information used by the app

  • Your creations: drawing strokes, selected templates, backgrounds, instruments, musical keys, tempo, drafts, thumbnails, and exported MIDI, WAV, or MP4 files. The app saves and processes these locally; it does not provide an account or a developer-operated cloud gallery.
  • Preferences and access: language, onboarding status, reminder preferences, rewarded-ad unlock progress and expiry times, and purchase entitlement information are stored locally to keep your settings and access working.
  • Support messages: if you contact us, we receive the email address and information you choose to include. Please avoid sending sensitive information that is not needed for support.

We use this information to provide drawing and playback, save and export work, remember preferences, manage access to features, and respond to support requests.

2. Advertising and third-party services

The app uses CAS.AI for advertising mediation. CAS and participating advertising partners may process IP addresses, mobile advertising identifiers, app-use information, and ad impressions or clicks. This supports ad delivery, measurement, reporting, and fraud prevention. Data practices depend on the networks serving ads and your consent choices.

Advertising services can receive information directly through their SDKs. Where required, consent choices are presented through the advertising consent flow. Available privacy options can be accessed from the app’s Settings. You can also review Android’s advertising privacy controls. Withholding permission for personalized advertising does not necessarily stop contextual advertising or necessary processing.

Premium access removes full-screen advertising and rewarded-ad requirements while the entitlement is active. Banner advertising remains visible, and associated advertising processing may continue.

Read the CAS.AI privacy policy and the Google privacy policy for their practices and available rights. Advertising partners may process information outside your country under their own policies and applicable safeguards.

3. Purchases and subscriptions

Google Play processes optional one-time purchases and weekly or monthly subscriptions. The app receives purchase identifiers, tokens, purchase status, and related entitlement information to verify, acknowledge, and restore purchases. We do not receive your full payment-card number or Google account password.

You can manage or cancel subscriptions through Google Play. Deleting app data or uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription or erase Google Play’s transaction records.

4. Permissions and notifications

  • Notifications: with your permission, the app can send creative reminders. You may decline notification permission and continue creating.
  • Internet and network status: used for advertising, consent, Google Play billing, and opening external links.
  • Device restart and time changes: used to restore reminder scheduling when applicable.
  • Media exports: exported files are saved through Android storage mechanisms and can be opened or shared with apps you choose.

The current drawing features do not require camera, microphone, contacts, or precise-location permission. Generated audio is not a microphone recording; exported drawing videos are generated from your creation. If you share a file or open it in another app, that app’s privacy practices apply.

5. Your choices and privacy requests

  • Turn Creative reminder off in Settings, or disable the app’s notifications in Android settings.
  • Review available ad privacy choices in the app and advertising controls in Android settings.
  • Choose whether to purchase premium access or watch optional rewarded ads.
  • Delete individual or multiple drafts and exports from the app’s Library.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or portability of personal information, or to object to certain processing or withdraw consent. Contact us using the details below. We may need information to verify a request. We cannot retrieve drawings stored only on your device. For data independently held by Google or advertising providers, their privacy request channels may be needed. You may also contact your relevant data protection authority.

6. Retention and deletion

Drafts, preferences, and unlock records remain on your device until removed, reset, or cleared. Deleting items from the app’s Library removes the app-managed items and their associated files where Android permits it. Copies previously shared, copied elsewhere, or retained by another app are not deleted by this action.

Use Android Settings → Apps → Draw Musical → Storage → Clear data to reset local app data. Uninstalling removes app-private data, but exported media in shared storage may remain and can be deleted using your file or media app. Android backup or device-transfer services may retain or restore eligible app data according to your device settings and the app’s backup configuration.

Support correspondence is retained only as needed to address the request and applicable recordkeeping obligations. Google and advertising providers retain data under their own policies, including any legal, security, or transaction retention requirements.

7. Security

We rely on Android’s app storage protections and Google Play’s purchase systems, and take reasonable steps to protect information we handle. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Protect your device and be careful when sharing exported files or support information.

8. Children’s privacy

The app does not require users to provide a name, email address, or account to draw and create music. Parents and guardians should supervise children’s use, including purchases, advertising interactions, and sharing. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through support or another direct interaction, contact us so we can review the request and remove information where required. Advertising providers’ processing is described in their linked policies.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when app features or data practices change. The updated policy will show a new date. If additional notice or consent is required, we will provide it through appropriate channels.
